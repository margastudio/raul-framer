History


Born in Buenos Aires, Argentina, Raúl Pardeilhan is an actor, model, DJ, producer, and entrepreneur with Argentinian and Italian heritage.



Born in Buenos Aires, Argentina, Raúl Pardeilhan is an actor, model, DJ, producer, and entrepreneur with Argentinian and Italian heritage.



His international career began in Buenos Aires before moving to Milan, Italy, where he spent five years working with some of the country's leading fashion and luxury brands. This experience established the foundation for a career that would later expand across Europe, the Americas, and Asia.




His international career began in Buenos Aires before moving to Milan, Italy, where he spent five years working with some of the country's leading fashion and luxury brands. This experience established the foundation for a career that would later expand across Europe, the Americas, and Asia.



For over a decade, Raúl has been based in China, where he founded Shining Tigers Production in Hong Kong. Throughout his career, he has collaborated with global brands, Fortune 500 companies, fashion houses, and international media productions as both on-screen talent and creative producer.



For over a decade, Raúl has been based in China, where he founded Shining Tigers Production in Hong Kong. Throughout his career, he has collaborated with global brands, Fortune 500 companies, fashion houses, and international media productions as both on-screen talent and creative producer.



Working across fashion, entertainment, corporate events, and music, Raúl has built a multidisciplinary career that combines creativity with business vision.

His experience spans campaigns, television, film, live events, and international productions in countries including Italy, France, Germany, Switzerland, the United States, Argentina, Uruguay, Thailand, the Philippines, China, and Hong Kong.

Shining Tigers Production:

Working for the biggest retailers like Zara and HM, thanks for our creative approach to catch the attention of the customers with live tailor made music for each brand to create the perfect atmosphere for the best experience possible at the retail stores. With an enthusiastic and welcoming approach being responsable for many opening ceremonies, launching new collections and Promotional Campaigns in the biggest city Centers in China including Wuhan, Xian, Chongqing, Zhuhai, HK, Macau, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Zhongshan, Beijing, Shanghai

Micromovie for Motivi Ms. Mo Xiao Qi, chinese famous actress and emcee and Raul Pardeilhan were starring the micro film: The Marriage Date in Rome which was directed by Hong Kong famous director Mr. Qian Guo Wei.

April 2016: Co-producer and partner with the Argentina Embassy for the Malbec World Day held at The Canton Tower in Guangzhou, being in charge of the music and dance shows and performances.

June 2016: Co-producer for the 200 years of Independence of the Argentina Republic celebrated at the Canton Club in Guangzhou.
