Who is Raul ?

Raul Pardeilhan is an international model, actor, director and creative entrepreneur whose work spans film, fashion, visual storytelling and purpose-driven innovation. With more than two decades of experience across Europe, Australia and Asia, he brings together cinematic vision, performance and global creative leadership.

More About Me

a passionate model, actor, and film director dedicated to bringing stories to life through performance and visual storytelling. Yes, I have worked with a range of brands and photographers, from commercial fashion to editorial and runway. I adapt to various styles and concepts, always looking to collaborate on creative projects that highlight both the clothing and the unique vibe of each brand.
